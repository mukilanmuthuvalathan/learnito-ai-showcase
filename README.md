# Learnito AI

[Open Learnito AI](https://learnitoai.vercel.app/)

Learnito AI is a study companion that helps students turn lecture notes and textbook material into summaries, important concepts, and practice quiz questions.

## Features

- Generate study guides from pasted study material.
- Review key summaries and important concepts.
- Practise with questions and short answers based on your notes.
- Save study guides locally for offline revision on the same browser and device.
- Use a 25-minute focus timer to organise study sessions.

## Get started

Visit [Learnito AI](https://learnitoai.vercel.app/), paste your study material, and select Generate. Review the guide against your original source and save it for later revision.
